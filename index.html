Hey there, Thanks for stopping by.

I'm Isra Aman Aziz, a bioinformatics researcher with a background in microbiology. My research focus majorly lies in identifying microbial( gut microbes particularly) and molecular signatures, biomarker discovery, genomics, and studying functional pathways with respect to human health, particularly in neurobiology by leveraging bioinformatics, integrating AI/ML and other cool tech tools.

I started out in microbiology because I was fascinated by the world that exists beyond what the human eye can see. In the lab, I found myself obsessing over documenting, sketching, analysing patterns, and trying to link these patterns into a system. Over time, I realized that what I loved most wasn't these organisms themselves but the systems behind them.

Today, I work with biological data using computational tools to organize, analyze, and interpret complex information with the intention of bridging the gap between data and discovery.

Research Interests

  • Gut microbiome and host-microbe interactions
  • Biomarker discovery and molecular signatures
  • Functional pathway mapping in metabolic and neuroinflammatory diseases
  • Neurobiology and the gut-brain axis
  • Genomics, multi-omics data integration and AI/ML applications in translational bioinformatics

Questions I keep coming back to:

  • How does diet reshape our gut microbiome, and how do the resulting metabolites, like SCFAs, tryptophan derivatives, bile acids feed back into neurobiological signaling?
  • How do we move biomarker discovery from correlation in cohort studies to something a clinician could actually act on, more like turn into a translational research?
  • Where does FMT genuinely belong in 2026 ofc beyond C. difficile and where is it still outrunning the mechanistic evidence, particularly for neuropsychiatric and neuroinflammatory indications?
  • If FMT works for some neurological or metabolic phenotypes, is it the whole community that matters, or a handful of keystone taxa and their metabolites and could we skip the transplant entirely with defined consortia or postbiotics

This website is my space to document my journey of learning, building, breaking, debugging and figuring things out along the way.
